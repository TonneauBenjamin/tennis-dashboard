# Storyboard — The Resilient Racket

**Audience:** FFT, regional tennis leagues, local authorities funding courts.
**One-sentence story:** Tennis was one of France's most COVID-proof club sports and grew three times faster than other sports afterwards, driven by young adults, but the boom did not close the gender gap or reach priority neighbourhoods.
